# Brand Color Palettes

Category bank and inspiration for step 2 of the `brand-definition` skill. Choose categories based on the agreed tone.

## Tech Companies (Inspiration Only)

- **Slack-like**: Primary `#611F69` (Deep Purple), Accent `#E01E5A` (Coral), Text `#FFFFFF`
- **Discord-like**: Primary `#5865F2` (Blurple), Accent `#57F287` (Green), Text `#FFFFFF`
- **Vercel-like**: Primary `#000000` (Black), Accent `#FFFFFF` (White), Text `#FFFFFF`
- **Stripe-like**: Primary `#635BFF` (Iris Blue), Accent `#D0D4FF` (Lavender), Text `#FFFFFF`

## Modern Startups

- **Warm & Energetic**: Primary `#FF6B35` (Coral), Accent `#FFA500` (Orange), Text `#FFFFFF` or `#2D3436`
- **Cool & Professional**: Primary `#0066CC` (Cobalt Blue), Accent `#00B4D8` (Sky Blue), Text `#FFFFFF`
- **Vibrant & Bold**: Primary `#7928CA` (Purple), Accent `#FF0080` (Pink), Text `#FFFFFF`

## Nature & Wellness

- **Forest Theme**: Primary `#2D6A4F` (Forest Green), Accent `#52B788` (Sage Green), Text `#FFFFFF`
- **Ocean Theme**: Primary `#0077B6` (Deep Blue), Accent `#00B4D8` (Aquamarine), Text `#FFFFFF`

## Gradients

- **Sunset**: From `#FF6B6B` to `#FFA500` (Text `#FFFFFF`)
- **Ocean**: From `#0077B6` to `#00B4D8` (Text `#FFFFFF`)
- **Forest**: From `#2D6A4F` to `#52B788` (Text `#FFFFFF`)
- **Twilight**: From `#5865F2` to `#7928CA` (Text `#FFFFFF`)
